# Scientific and Attribution Notice

This web project implements the Yallop lunar crescent visibility q-test for educational, analytical, and research use.

Primary method reference:

B. D. Yallop, *A Method for Predicting the First Sighting of the New Crescent Moon*, NAO Technical Note No. 69, HM Nautical Almanac Office, 1997; updated 1998.

The project is an independent implementation and is not an official HM Nautical Almanac Office or UK Hydrographic Office product.

Predictions are probabilistic. Actual observations depend on atmospheric transparency, local horizon, observer experience, elevation, optical equipment, and other conditions.

Prepared and designed by physicist Omar Albaz, Iraq.
